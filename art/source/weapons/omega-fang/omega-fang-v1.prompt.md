## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original 2D pixel-art game weapon, separate transparent sprite concept. Create ONE excellent compact sci-fi samurai sword for Marty in Omega armor, a short broad single-edged energy saber named conceptually Omega Fang (NO written text). It must match ivory armor, chunky gold trim, cyan visor and purple cape. Orthographic flat side view, perfectly horizontal with pommel LEFT and blade tip RIGHT, entire weapon visible with ample transparent padding. Dark indigo wrapped substantial ergonomic handle occupying 22 percent of length, small angular gold tsuba guard with cyan circular reactor, ivory mechanical blade spine and bold luminous cyan cutting edge, chisel/katana tip, slight elegant upward curve. Clean aggressive readable silhouette, practical substantial hand grip, not a huge ornamental greatsword. Pixel-art aesthetic with deliberate crisp square pixel clusters, dark navy outline, limited palette, bright white/cyan highlights, gold shadows. No soft painterly texture, no motion trails, no bloom cloud, no gradients into the background, no shadows on floor, NO hand, NO person, no duplicate views, no labels, no background, real alpha transparency. This will be carried in hand independently from character sprite and rotated around the handle. Make it look powerful and elegant but readable when reduced to roughly 72 pixels long. Center the single sword in landscape composition.
