--- ESTILO (colar no início de todos) ---
Style: hand-drawn anime/manga illustration in homage to Akira Toriyama's late-80s/90s color art
(Dragon Ball / Dr. Slump / Dragon Quest era): clean confident black ink lines with varied weight,
simple cel shading with one crisp shadow tone and a bright rim highlight, saturated flat colors,
big expressive eyes and friendly cocky grin, chunky rounded mechanical designs full of rivets,
vents, bolts and panel lines, playful dynamic pose, slight low-angle hero shot.
Character stands on a small hexagonal orange-and-dark-steel platform, feet visible, centered, full body,
filling about 80% of the height. Background: soft, painterly night harbor of 1941 London (brick
warehouses, dock cranes, distant fires and searchlights), out of focus, dark blue with warm orange
glows, with a large soft glow behind the character in the character's accent color.
Aspect 738x610 (landscape, a bit wider than tall). No text, no UI, no frame, no watermark.
